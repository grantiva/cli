OVERVIEW: Reply to a ticket as the team. Moves it to awaiting_reply and emails
the submitter if that notification is on.

USAGE: grantiva console support reply [--json] [--verbose] [--quiet] <ticket> --body <body>

ARGUMENTS:
  <ticket>                Ticket ID.

OPTIONS:
  --json                  Output as JSON
  --verbose               Print diagnostic detail (timestamps, labels,
                          metadata) to stderr.
  --quiet                 Silence progress diagnostics on stderr; warnings and
                          errors still print. Program output on stdout is
                          unaffected.
  --body <body>           Reply text, inline or @file.
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
