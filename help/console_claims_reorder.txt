OVERVIEW: Set claim priorities by listing every claim in order.

Every claim must appear exactly once; the first listed gets priority 0.

USAGE: grantiva console claims reorder [--json] [--verbose] [--quiet] <claims> ...

ARGUMENTS:
  <claims>                Claim keys or UUIDs, in the desired order.

OPTIONS:
  --json                  Output as JSON
  --verbose               Print diagnostic detail (timestamps, labels,
                          metadata) to stderr.
  --quiet                 Silence progress diagnostics on stderr; warnings and
                          errors still print. Program output on stdout is
                          unaffected.
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
