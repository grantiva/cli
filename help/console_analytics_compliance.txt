OVERVIEW: Compliance rate, violation counts, and the devices that are out of
compliance.

USAGE: grantiva console analytics compliance [--json] [--verbose] [--quiet] [--period <period>] [--all]

OPTIONS:
  --json                  Output as JSON
  --verbose               Print diagnostic detail (timestamps, labels,
                          metadata) to stderr.
  --quiet                 Silence progress diagnostics on stderr; warnings and
                          errors still print. Program output on stdout is
                          unaffected.
  --period <period>       Reporting period label: 1d, 7d, 30d, or 90d. Default
                          30d. (values: 1d, 7d, 30d, 90d)
  --all                   List every device, not just the non-compliant ones.
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
