OVERVIEW: Plan and billing period (read-only; manage billing in the dashboard).

USAGE: grantiva console org billing <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  show (default)          Show the plan, usage, and current period.

  See 'grantiva help console org billing <subcommand>' for detailed help.
