OVERVIEW: Risk alert rules, the attestation failure-rate alert, and
notification preferences.

USAGE: grantiva console alerts <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  rules                   Risk alert rules (Business plan and up).
  failure-rate            The attestation failure-rate alert.
  notifications           Which events email the org admin.

  See 'grantiva help console alerts <subcommand>' for detailed help.
