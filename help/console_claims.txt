OVERVIEW: Manage the custom claims minted into device JWTs.

USAGE: grantiva console claims <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  list                    List custom claims in priority order.
  get                     Show one claim by key or UUID, including its
                          configuration.
  create                  Create a custom claim.
  update                  Change a claim's name, description, priority, active
                          state, or configuration.
  delete                  Delete a claim.
  reorder                 Set claim priorities by listing every claim in order.
  test                    Evaluate a claim definition against a simulated
                          device without saving it.
  preview                 Evaluate a saved claim against a simulated device.

  See 'grantiva help console claims <subcommand>' for detailed help.
