OVERVIEW: Review one screen of a run.

USAGE: grantiva console vrt screen <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  accept                  Accept a screen's new capture; it becomes the
                          baseline on approval.
  flag                    Flag a screen as a real regression; the old baseline
                          is kept.
  reset                   Clear a screen's review decision.

  See 'grantiva help console vrt screen <subcommand>' for detailed help.
