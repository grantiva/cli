OVERVIEW: Manage feature flags.

USAGE: grantiva console flags <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  list                    List all feature flags.
  get                     Show one flag in detail, including rules and
                          overrides.
  create                  Create a feature flag.
  update                  Update a flag's name, description, or per-environment
                          values.
  on                      Turn a flag on.
  off                     Turn a flag off.
  delete                  Delete a flag, its rules, and its overrides.
  rules                   Manage a flag's targeting rules.
  overrides               Manage per-device flag overrides.
  eval                    Dry-run a flag against a simulated device and show
                          the full rule trace.
  history                 Show a flag's change history.
  evaluations             Show a flag's most recent recorded evaluations.
  watch                   Stream live flag configuration updates (SSE). Ctrl-C
                          to stop.

  See 'grantiva help console flags <subcommand>' for detailed help.
