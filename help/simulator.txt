OVERVIEW: Provision, inspect, and tear down managed simulators.

USAGE: grantiva simulator <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  ensure                  Create or reuse a named simulator and boot it.
                          `--name` alone is enough: the device type is read
                          from the name and the newest installed runtime is
                          used.
  delete
  sessions
  teardown                End a managed session, or reclaim one simulator by
                          UDID when no ledger entry exists.
  cleanup                 Delete Grantiva-created simulators that are shut down
                          and not part of an active session.

  See 'grantiva help simulator <subcommand>' for detailed help.
