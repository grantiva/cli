OVERVIEW: Risk alert rules (Business plan and up).

USAGE: grantiva console alerts rules <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  list                    List risk alert rules.
  get                     Show one risk alert rule.
  create                  Alert a webhook when a device's risk score crosses a
                          threshold.
  update                  Change a rule's name, threshold, comparison, URL, or
                          active state.
  delete                  Delete a risk alert rule.
  deliveries              Recent risk alert deliveries across all rules.

  See 'grantiva help console alerts rules <subcommand>' for detailed help.
