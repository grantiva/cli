OVERVIEW: Attestation analytics: overview, event log, risk and compliance
reports, CSV export.

USAGE: grantiva console analytics <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  overview                Attestation totals, success rate, unique devices,
                          average risk, and the latest events.
  events                  Page through the attestation event log.
  device                  Show one device's risk profile, compliance status,
                          and recent events.
  risk                    Risk distribution, critical-risk devices, and recent
                          suspicious activity.
  compliance              Compliance rate, violation counts, and the devices
                          that are out of compliance.
  export                  Export devices or events as CSV, to stdout or a file.

  See 'grantiva help console analytics <subcommand>' for detailed help.
