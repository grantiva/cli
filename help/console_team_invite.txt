OVERVIEW: Email an invite to join the organization.

USAGE: grantiva console team invite [--json] [--verbose] [--quiet] <email> [--role <role>]

ARGUMENTS:
  <email>                 Email address.

OPTIONS:
  --json                  Output as JSON
  --verbose               Print diagnostic detail (timestamps, labels,
                          metadata) to stderr.
  --quiet                 Silence progress diagnostics on stderr; warnings and
                          errors still print. Program output on stdout is
                          unaffected.
  --role <role>           Role: viewer, member, or admin. Default member.
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
