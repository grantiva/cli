OVERVIEW: Organization settings.

USAGE: grantiva console org settings <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  get                     Show organization settings.
  set-name                Rename the organization. The slug follows the name.

  See 'grantiva help console org settings <subcommand>' for detailed help.
