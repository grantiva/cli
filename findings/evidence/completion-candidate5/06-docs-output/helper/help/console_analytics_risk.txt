OVERVIEW: Risk distribution, critical-risk devices, and recent suspicious
activity.

USAGE: grantiva console analytics risk [--json] [--verbose] [--quiet] [--period <period>]

OPTIONS:
  --json                  Output as JSON
  --verbose               Print diagnostic detail (timestamps, labels,
                          metadata) to stderr.
  --quiet                 Silence progress diagnostics on stderr; warnings and
                          errors still print. Program output on stdout is
                          unaffected.
  --period, --range <period>
                          Window: 1d, 7d, 30d, or 90d. Default 7d. (--range is
                          retained as an alias.) (values: 1d, 7d, 30d, 90d)
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
