OVERVIEW: Manage your Grantiva dashboard from the terminal.

USAGE: grantiva console <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  flags, featureflags     Manage feature flags.
  envs                    Manage flag environments (production, staging, ...).
  analytics               Attestation analytics: overview, event log, risk and
                          compliance reports, CSV export.
  devices                 Attested devices: list, filter, and inspect by key ID.
  apps                    Register and manage the apps (bundle IDs) attesting
                          under this organization.
  claims                  Manage the custom claims minted into device JWTs.
  vrt                     Review visual regression runs: approve, reject, and
                          accept or flag individual screens.
  releases                Author the What's New release notes shown to users
                          after they upgrade.
  feedback                Triage feature requests: list, read the thread, set
                          status, reply as the team.
  support                 Work support tickets: list, read the conversation,
                          reply, set status and priority.
  webhooks                Manage webhook endpoints and inspect their deliveries.
  alerts                  Risk alert rules, the attestation failure-rate alert,
                          and notification preferences.
  keys                    Manage API keys. A key can only create keys with
                          scopes it holds itself.
  team                    Members and invites.
  audit                   The organization audit log.
  org                     Organization settings, usage, and billing.
  open                    Open the dashboard in your browser, optionally at an
                          area.

  See 'grantiva help console <subcommand>' for detailed help.
