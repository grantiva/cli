OVERVIEW: Manage a flag's targeting rules.

Rules are evaluated in priority order; the first rule whose conditions all pass
(and whose rollout bucket accepts the device) decides the flag's value.

Conditions are given as repeated --when options in the form
<attribute>:<operator>:<value>, or as a --conditions-json array. Operators: eq,
neq, gt, gte, lt, lte, in, not_in, contains, starts_with. For in/not_in,
comma-separate the values:

  --when os_version:gte:18.0 --when country:in:US,CA

Attributes: os_version, device_model, app_version, risk_score, locale, country,
device_id, user_id, attestation_status, or custom.<key>.

USAGE: grantiva console flags rules <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  list                    List a flag's targeting rules in priority order.
  add                     Add a targeting rule to a flag (appended at lowest
                          priority).
  update                  Update a targeting rule.
  delete                  Delete a targeting rule.
  reorder                 Reorder a flag's rules.

  See 'grantiva help console flags rules <subcommand>' for detailed help.
