OVERVIEW: Attested devices: list, filter, and inspect by key ID.

USAGE: grantiva console devices <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  list                    List devices, most recently attested first.
  get                     Show a device's profile and its recent attestation
                          events.

  See 'grantiva help console devices <subcommand>' for detailed help.
