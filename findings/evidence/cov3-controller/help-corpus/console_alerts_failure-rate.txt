OVERVIEW: The attestation failure-rate alert.

USAGE: grantiva console alerts failure-rate <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  get                     Show the failure-rate alert settings.
  set                     Enable, disable, or tune the failure-rate alert.
  history                 Times the failure-rate alert fired.

  See 'grantiva help console alerts failure-rate <subcommand>' for detailed help.
