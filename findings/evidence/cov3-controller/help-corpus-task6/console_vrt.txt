OVERVIEW: Review visual regression runs: approve, reject, and accept or flag
individual screens.

`grantiva ci run` creates and completes a run. These commands close the loop
from a pipeline: review the screens that changed, then approve (promoting
accepted captures to baselines) or reject.

USAGE: grantiva console vrt <subcommand>

OPTIONS:
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.

SUBCOMMANDS:
  runs                    List or inspect runs.
  approve                 Approve a run: accepted screens become the branch
                          baselines.
  reject                  Reject a run. Baselines are untouched.
  screen                  Review one screen of a run.

  See 'grantiva help console vrt <subcommand>' for detailed help.
