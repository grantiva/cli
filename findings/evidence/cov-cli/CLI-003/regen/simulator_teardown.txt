OVERVIEW: End a managed session, or reclaim one simulator by UDID when no
ledger entry exists.

USAGE: grantiva simulator teardown [--json] [--verbose] [--quiet] [--session-id <session-id>] [--udid <udid>] [--force]

OPTIONS:
  --json                  Output as JSON
  --verbose               Print diagnostic detail (timestamps, labels,
                          metadata) to stderr.
  --quiet                 Silence progress diagnostics on stderr; warnings and
                          errors still print. Program output on stdout is
                          unaffected.
  --session-id <session-id>
                          Ticket/session identifier to shut down and release.
  --udid <udid>           Simulator UDID to reclaim. Use with --force when a
                          run was killed and left processes owning the device.
  --force                 Reclaim by live process inspection instead of the
                          session ledger: kills the grantiva-runner,
                          WebDriverAgent xcodebuild, and simctl diagnose
                          processes holding the simulator, breaks the lease,
                          and reconciles the session registry.
  --version               Show the version.
  -h, --help              Show help information.
